Premium Facilities at IPL Venues

World-class facilities ensuring comfort, convenience, and exceptional experience for players, officials, media, and spectators at all IPL stadiums during the tournament.

Seating & Comfort

  • Premium VIP Boxes: Air-conditioned, private viewing areas with premium seating
  • Hospitality Suites: Gourmet dining experiences with stadium views
  • General Seating: Cushioned seats with excellent sightlines throughout stadium
  • Accessible Seating: Wheelchair-accessible areas with companion seating
  • Family Zones: Entertainment areas designed for children and families

Food & Beverage Services

  • Multiple dining outlets: Regional and international cuisines available
  • Beverage stations: Cold drinks, juices, and hot beverages throughout venue
  • Quick snack corners: Popcorn, nachos, ice cream, and fast food options
  • Premium restaurants: Fine dining experiences for special events
  • Vendor stands: Street food and local specialties for authentic experience

Entertainment & Services

  • Large LED Display Screens: Live match coverage and instant replays
  • High-Speed WiFi Connectivity: Free internet throughout stadium
  • Mobile App Integration: Real-time updates and merchandise ordering
  • Professional Photography Services: High-quality match photographs available
  • Lost & Found: Efficient item retrieval and customer service

Health & Safety

  • Medical facilities: On-site clinics and ambulances on standby
  • Fire safety: Modern sprinkler systems and emergency equipment
  • Security personnel: Trained crowd management and security staff
  • First aid kits: Strategically located first aid stations
  • Emergency exits: Multiple evacuation routes and clear signage
