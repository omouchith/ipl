How to Reach IPL Stadiums

Comprehensive travel guide with transportation options, parking information, and accessibility details for all IPL venues across India.

Mumbai (Wankhede Stadium)

  • Airport: Indira Gandhi International - 20 km away (45 mins drive)
  • Railway: Mumbai Central Station - 2 km distance
  • Metro: Marine Lines Metro Station - 1 km walk
  • Parking: 3,000+ parking spaces | Premium: ₹500
  • Local Transport: Taxi, auto-rickshaw, local buses available

Chennai (M.A. Chidambaram Stadium)

  • Airport: Chennai International - 14 km (30 mins travel)
  • Railway: Chennai Central Station - 2 km distance
  • Metro: Chepauk Metro Station - Adjacent to stadium
  • Parking: 2,500+ spaces available | ₹300 general, ₹600 premium
  • Accessibility: Well-connected public transportation network

Kolkata (Eden Gardens)

  • Airport: Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International - 20 km
  • Railway: Howrah Station - 4 km from venue
  • Tram/Metro: Rabindra Sarovar Metro Station - 1.5 km
  • Parking: 5,000+ spaces | General parking: ₹400
  • Attractions: Historic venues and tourist spots nearby

Travel Tips for IPL Matches

  • Arrive 2-3 hours before match start for smooth entry and parking
  • Avoid peak traffic hours; consider public transportation option
  • Download stadium mobile app for live navigation assistance
  • Park early for best parking rates and availability
  • Use ride-sharing apps for convenient door-to-door transport
