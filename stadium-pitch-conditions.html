Pitch Analysis - All IPL Venues 2026

Each stadium has unique pitch characteristics that influence team selection, strategy, and match outcomes significantly. Understanding these conditions is crucial for match prediction.

Venue-Specific Pitch Reports

  • Wankhede (Mumbai): Batting paradise | Fast-paced, hard surface | Average score: 178
  • M.A. Chidambaram (Chennai): Spinner's haven | Slow, turning pitch | Average score: 162
  • Eden Gardens (Kolkata): Balanced conditions | Good for all-rounders | Average score: 170
  • Arun Jaitley (Delhi): Pacer-friendly | Hard pitch, pace and bounce | Average score: 165
  • Narendra Modi (Ahmedabad): Fastest ground | Highest boundaries | Average score: 185

Pitch Behavior During Season

  • Early Season (March-April): Fresh pitches, more even contests between bat and ball
  • Mid Season (May): Worn-out surfaces, favor spinners more prominently
  • Late Season (June): Deteriorated pitches, unpredictable and challenging behavior
  • Evening Matches: Dew factor reduces turn and bounce significantly
  • Day Matches: Firmer pitches provide better batting conditions

Team Preferences by Pitch Type

  • Batting-Friendly Pitches: RCB & MI excel | Average: 180+ runs per team
  • Spin-Friendly Pitches: CSK advantage | Average: 165 runs
  • Balanced Pitches: All teams remain competitive | Average: 170 runs
  • Fast-Bowling Pitches: SRH & DC preferred | Average: 160 runs

Boundary Variations Impact

  • Shortest Boundary: Wankhede (62m in one direction)
  • Largest Boundaries: Eden Gardens, Arun Jaitley (75m+)
  • Most Sixes Hit: Narendra Modi Stadium (3.2 sixes/match average)
  • Boundary Percentage Impact: 15-20% variation in match outcomes
