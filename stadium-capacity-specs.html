Stadium Capacity and Technical Specifications

Detailed information about stadium capacity, dimensions, infrastructure quality, and technical specifications of all IPL venues for comprehensive venue comparison.

Capacity Rankings

  • 1. Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad: 132,000 (World's Largest Cricket Stadium)
  • 2. Eden Gardens, Kolkata: 68,000 (Second Largest)
  • 3. Bharat Ratna Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Stadium: 54,000 (Modern facility)
  • 4. M.A. Chidambaram Stadium: 38,000 (Historic ground)
  • 5. M. Chinnaswamy Stadium: 38,000 (High-altitude venue)

Pitch Dimensions

  • Standard Field Length: 150 meters (approximately all venues)
  • Boundary Length (Average): 70-75 meters from stumps
  • Pitch Length: 22 yards (Standard cricket pitch - universal)
  • Surface Area: Cricket ground approximately 1.5-2 acres
  • Playing Area: 120-130 meters coverage typically

Infrastructure Quality 2026

  • HD Broadcast Facilities: 4K cameras, drone coverage, multiple angles
  • VIP Seating: Climate-controlled luxury boxes with premium hospitality
  • Practice Grounds: Indoor and outdoor nets for team preparation
  • Hospitality Services: World-class food and beverage facilities
  • Security Systems: State-of-the-art CCTV and advanced crowd management

Renovation Highlights 2026

  • LED Floodlights upgrade in 5 major venues for enhanced visibility
  • Enhanced drainage systems for all-weather play capability
  • Accessibility improvements for differently-abled visitors
  • Improved parking infrastructure at all stadiums
