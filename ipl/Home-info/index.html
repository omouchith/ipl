Our Story

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is one of the biggest and most popular cricket leagues in the world. Founded by the BCCI in 2007, the tournament brings together international stars and young talents to compete in an exciting T20 format.

Tournament Format

  • 10 Teams participate in the tournament.
  • Each team plays 14 league matches.
  • Top 4 teams qualify for the Playoffs.
  • The Final determines the IPL Champion.
