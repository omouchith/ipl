IPL 2026 - Venue Information

The Indian Premier League showcases cricket across India's most prestigious stadiums. Each venue has unique characteristics, pitch behavior, and hosting capabilities that add character to the tournament.

Primary Stadiums Hosting IPL 2026

  • Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai (MI): Capacity 33,108 | Batting-friendly pitch | Home of Bollywood
  • M.A. Chidambaram Stadium, Chennai (CSK): Capacity 38,000 | Spin-friendly | Historic venue
  • Eden Gardens, Kolkata (KKR): Capacity 68,000 | Balanced conditions | Ancient ground
  • Arun Jaitley Stadium, Delhi (DC): Capacity 41,820 | Pace-friendly | Modern facility
  • Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad (GT): Capacity 132,000 | Fastest ground | World's largest

Additional Venues

  • Sawai Mansingh Stadium, Jaipur (RR) - Capacity 30,000 | High altitude
  • Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Hyderabad (SRH) - Capacity 39,037 | Balanced
  • Punjab Cricket Association Stadium, Mohali (PBKS) - Capacity 26,000 | Cool evenings
  • Bharat Ratna Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Stadium, Ahmedabad (LSG) - Capacity 54,000 | Modern tech
  • M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bangalore (RCB) - Capacity 38,000 | High-altitude

Stadium Characteristics & Advantages

  • High-altitude venues: Jaipur, Bangalore - Ball travels faster
  • Coastal venues: Chennai, Mumbai - Pitch characteristics vary with seasons
  • Indoor facilities: Climate-controlled practice zones at all major venues
  • International standards: FIFA certified grounds with world-class infrastructure
